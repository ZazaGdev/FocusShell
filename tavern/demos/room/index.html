Pixel Tavern: the room furnished (#248, #249, #251, #261)

About this room

The furniture and props are the owner's Gemini pieces, one picture each, cleaned into pixel art and brought to the people's scale; floor and walls are drawn in code. The room is 27 by 14 tiles (1296 by 672 pixels). A rustic corner bar with beer taps closes the left side and is open on the right; the tables are mixed (a long table for four, square and round tables for two, small round tables for one), 20 seats in all, with aisles between. The barkeep is the owner's likeness in barkeep clothes (#259); the 18 robots sit a while, walk the aisles to stand at the bar, and come back. The full-size characters are drawn 1.25 times their art (#260); the helpers are about three quarters of a robot (#263). Seven of them (#262: barkeep, CTO, PM, FocusShell, AI Fun, Compass, Budget) turn to their side when walking left or right and show their back walking away; the CTO has a back view only. The fire, candles and torches are animated from their frames; the arched windows and the room light follow the local clock: day from 7:00, dusk from 18:00, night from 21:00 (dusk again from 5:00).
